## Chapter 12: Pointers and Arrays

Building upon pointer fundamentals, this chapter begins by examining **Pointer Arithmetic Operations**, showing how offsets, differences, and comparisons navigate contiguous memory.

Next, it applies arithmetic in **Array Traversal with Pointers**, detailing efficient loop constructs and combining indirection with increment operators like `*p++`.

Then, it explains the core language duality in **The Array-Pointer Relationship**, establishing array decay, subscript equivalence, and pointer-equivalent array arguments.

Finally, it expands to matrix architectures in **Pointers with Multidimensional Arrays**, covering flattened row-major traversal and typed pointers to fixed-length array blocks.
