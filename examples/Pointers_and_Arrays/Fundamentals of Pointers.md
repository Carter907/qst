## Fundamentals of Pointers

Understanding pointers begins with visualizing computer architecture through **Memory Address and Byte Organization**, where memory is treated as a continuous sequence of numbered byte cells.

To work with these hardware locations directly in C, authors employ **Pointer Variable Declaration** to create typed variables that reference and navigate memory.
