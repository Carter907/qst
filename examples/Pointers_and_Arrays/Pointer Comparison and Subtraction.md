## Pointer Comparison and Subtraction

Subtracting two pointers that address elements within the same array computes their **Pointer Difference**, yielding the count of intervening elements.

Relational expressions perform **Pointer Comparison** to establish precedence order in loops, determining whether one pointer is positioned earlier or later in memory than another.
