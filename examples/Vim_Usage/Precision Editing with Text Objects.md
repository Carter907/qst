## Precision Editing with Text Objects

Semantic text manipulation starts with **Word Text Objects**, modifying inner words and spacing boundaries regardless of cursor placement.

Surrounding enclosures and strings are targeted via **Delimiter and Quote Text Objects**, empowering swift mutations within quotes and parentheses.

For larger structural components, **Block and Tag Text Objects** operate directly on markup tags, paragraphs, and multi-line indented blocks.
