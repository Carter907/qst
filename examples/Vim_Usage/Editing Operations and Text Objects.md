## Editing Operations and Text Objects

The expressive language of Vim begins with **Operator Motion Grammar**, uniting action verbs with directional targets and repeatable dot execution.

Practical editing workflows are driven by **Core Deletion and Modification**, managing text deletions, in-line replacements, and yank/put mechanics.

Speed reaches professional levels when employing **Precision Editing with Text Objects**, mutating text directly inside quotes, brackets, and markup tags.
