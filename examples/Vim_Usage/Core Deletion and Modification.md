## Core Deletion and Modification

Buffer pruning and cutting operations are governed by **Delete and Cut Operations**, capturing removed text directly into registers.

Merging deletion and insertion into an atomic operation is performed via **Change and Replace Operations**, supporting single character and block rewrites.

Non-destructive text duplication and insertion are facilitated by **Yank and Put Mechanics**, repositioning clipboard content with precision.
