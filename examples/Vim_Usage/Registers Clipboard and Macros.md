## Registers Clipboard and Macros

Multi-slot text preservation and external clipboard synchronization are handled by **Vim Registers and System Clipboard**, separating yanks from cuts.

Repetitive editing workflows are automated through **Macro Recording and Playback**, recording keystroke sequences for single-key reproduction.
