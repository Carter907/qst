## Vim Core Concepts and Navigation

The starting foundation of Vim fluency requires internalizing **Modal Architecture and State**, establishing mode-switching reflexes across Normal, Insert, Command, and Visual states.

Building upon modal discipline, **Basic and Word Motions** instills ergonomic cursor navigation on the home row and rapid word-boundary jumps.

Expanding precision across larger distances, **Targeted Line and Find Motions** enables rapid in-line search targeting and structural bracket navigation.
