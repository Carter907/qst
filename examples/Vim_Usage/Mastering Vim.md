## Mastering Vim

Mastering Vim begins by internalizing **Vim Core Concepts and Navigation**, establishing foundational modal discipline, ergonomic cursor movements, and rapid character targeting.

Expanding from movement into manipulation, **Editing Operations and Text Objects** articulates the expressive grammar of Vim, coupling verbs with precision nouns, text objects, and repeatable actions.

For scalable refactoring, **Search Substitution and Pattern Matching** equips you with forward/backward pattern search, options, and global range-based substitutions.

Finally, **Advanced Workflows and Environment Control** elevates your editing environment through multiple buffers, split windows, registers, macro automation, and custom `.vimrc` configurations.
